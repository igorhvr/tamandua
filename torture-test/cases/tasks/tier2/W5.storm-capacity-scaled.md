# W5: The storm — capacity-scaled, two rounds, one briefing (REAL, tt-poly-lite)

This task file IS the storm briefing (spec `09-wave-5-storm.md`). The
manifest row `W5.storm-capacity-scaled` anchors the wave-5 storm on the
**capacity-scaled** variant: a host whose profile cannot carry the full
8+2-run tt-poly roster runs the storm on **tt-poly-lite** — the
two-language storm monorepo (`python/` schedlib-poly + `ts/`
expense-tracker-poly) whose composite `seed/storm` ref layers every storm
seed onto the green baseline (see `fixtures-src/tt-poly-lite/seeds/SEEDS.md`
+ `seeds/storm/STORM.md`). The fixture's canonical TEST_CMD is
`./run-all-tests` (~3–4 min with both suites bootstrapped); `python3` and
`node >= 22` are required (the manifest `requires` gates on exactly that).

The storm is **two rounds, not one** (attribution is the point): a failure
under 8-concurrency-plus-chaos is uninterpretable. Round A is the clean
full roster (no injected faults, operators observe only); Round B is a
reduced 5-run roster plus the chaos schedule. Both rounds instantiate
worktrees/tasks from the composite `seed/storm` ref — one ref pinning every
storm task's bait, bug, and sentinel content so reruns are
byte-reproducible.

### Timer math (queue admission), pinned

Timers required = COUNT(DISTINCT agent_id) per run: fdmw=6, bfmw=6,
security-audit-mw=7, quarantine-mw=4, do-review-do-verify=3, do-now=1.
Active roster: 3×6 + 2×6 + 7 + 4 + 3 = **44**. The storm daemon's spawn env
sets `TAMANDUA_MAX_ACTIVE_TIMERS=44` — the limit is pinned so S9/S10 queue.
S9/S10 launch **immediately after S8's registration** (30s stagger for
S9/S10, before early completions can free timers). **Admission-decision
correctness assertion (snapshot-based):** at each of S9/S10's admission
attempts the orchestrator records `freeSlots`; the decision (queue vs
admit) must match the snapshot exactly. Expected shape: both queue, then
drain as capacity frees; if an early completion makes an instant admit
CORRECT, that is a pass of the correctness assertion — never a forced-queue
hope. S10 must be admitted within 10min of first capacity; nothing is lost
in `queued`.

### Simultaneity-manipulation check (gating the storm's claim)

The controller samples run/step states **every 15s** and must record at
least one window where **all 8 roster runs simultaneously hold a claimed or
running step**. If peak concurrency never reaches 8 (early completions,
serialization), the storm's headline is recorded as the **observed peak** —
reporting "8-run storm passed" at an actual peak of 5 is
self-manipulation; the roster is then re-tuned (longer tasks) for the rerun
or next campaign.

## Success bands (the orchestrator's acceptance criteria)

- **O2 union at each round's end:** final `main` contains every landed
  run's patch-id (S7's quarantine content excluded from main and present on
  `broken-tests`); full `./run-all-tests` green on final main; zero phantom
  merges; zero dropped sibling hunks — checked after EVERY landing from ref
  snapshots. Round B additionally: B4's landing carries its red-suite
  annotation trail (`landed_over_red_suite`).
- **Conflict-designated runs assessed separately** (S5/S9 pair; Round B:
  B3 after chaos #2): each must reach the rebase→re-test loop and land with
  re-attested TESTED_TREE, **or fail honestly with the target intact**.
- **Non-conflict-designated band (Round A):** of S1–S4, S6–S8, at most one
  honest failure; **≥6 of the 8 merge-eligible runs land** overall when
  conflict-designated successes are included — and Round A being
  fault-free, every non-landing needs a named, evidence-backed cause.
- Round A simultaneity: the 8-concurrent window observed and recorded (or
  the reduced peak honestly reported).
- Queue behavior (Round A): S9/S10 admitted only as capacity frees; S10
  within 10min of first capacity; nothing lost in `queued`.
- Chaos recoveries (Round B): B3 resumes and finishes; B4 recovers its
  story after the harness kill; B5 reaches stopped, deletes cleanly with
  zero residue (worktree, claims, timers), and its relaunch completes;
  nudge storm shows zero duplicate dispatch; mass rugpull recovers all four
  independently; PARK bait preserves the dirty tree byte-identical; daemon
  bounce loses nothing (all in-flight runs progress within 2 dispatch
  intervals). Post-bounce single-flight: no stuck waiter loops; the next
  same-key suite executes fresh; exit-87 rows exist for suites whose shims
  were torn down (assert the observable consequences, not the vanished map).
- Hygiene battery (O4, O8, O9 + post-batch O5–O7, O12 — the implemented
  per-case set is declared on the row; the not-yet-implemented O5–O7/O12
  and the O9 single-flight prelude are backstops named here, never declared
  in the manifest) clean at storm end.
- Token accounting: every terminal run attributed (O3z), including the
  canceled and chaos-killed rounds.
- **Wedge deadline within the campaign:** any run non-terminal at T+44h
  (2h before W6) is force-stopped (`workflow stop`, escalating per
  contract) and classified — a run that stops cleanly on command is an
  honest chaos casualty; a run that CANNOT be stopped is the S1 wedge.

  PID mid-execution on a second round — assert dead-owner reclaim in
  seconds. During the storm itself, O9 asserts only per-run ledger
  integrity and zero cross-run interference.
