# 04 — Wave 0: Preflight & Environment Gates (pre-day + T+0 → T+4h)

Zero-token (plus two small canaries). Every scenario here is a **gate**: a
failure blocks the campaign (fix, re-run W0) rather than consuming later
waves' budget on a broken foundation. The stale-dist and stale-catalog defect
classes have each independently destroyed full runs; W0 exists so they can't
destroy this campaign.

**Dress rehearsal:** the full W0 runs once on the day BEFORE T+0 (it is the
acceptance test of the tooling — P4 in 12). First-execution W0 realistically
costs 4–6h (cold maven caches, toolchain surprises, provisioning fixes);
absorbing that on the pre-day means campaign-day W0 is a 2–4h re-verify.
Environment fixes discovered here are pre-campaign work, not findings.
